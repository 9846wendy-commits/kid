Teaching Tips

"Look kids! This is an angry apple... making the /æ/ sound!"

For letter 'A', focus ONLY on the short /æ/ sound (like Apple, Ant). Avoid words like 'Alien' or 'Acorn' initially to prevent confusion for early learners.
